Why 64 words = 1.33 ms, not 0.67 ms: with S19 framing one 32-bit word holds L+R (one word per sample period),
so 64 words = 64 sample periods = 64/48000 s = 1.333 ms. The 0.67 ms figure assumes I2S with L and R in separate words (2 words/frame).

Target: <= 10 ms (S1, S6). Write FIFO adds up to 64 words because it 'attempts to stay filled' (S14 §2.4.4.1, p. 52).
