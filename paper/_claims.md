# Claims list for `paper/main.tex`

Every claim, number and comparison in the paper, with its evidence. Fact IDs (P1, H2, D8, B6, …) refer to `docs/_facts.md`, which names the original repository file.

**Evidence types:** BOARD = measured on the LCDK (`results/phase4/board/`); SIM = Python/MATLAB/PC-C; CALC = formula or model, not measured; LIT = published source (key in `refs.bib`); DESIGN = our choice; FIG = regenerated by `paper/make_figs.py` (which asserts the value).

Dropped for lack of evidence: measured latency; lip-sync argument; "most common hearing loss"; any clinical, real-ear or listener-study claim; any comparison of our numbers with the literature as "better"; laptop-vs-DSP timing.
